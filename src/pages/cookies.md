## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website can store on your browser or device. They can help a website function, remember a setting, measure performance, or support advertising. Similar technologies, such as local storage and tracking pixels, may serve comparable purposes.

## Managing cookies

Most browsers let you block, delete, or control cookies in their settings. Blocking all cookies may affect how some websites work. You can usually find controls in your browser’s privacy or security settings.

If we add non-essential cookies or similar technologies that require consent, we will provide a clear consent choice and update this policy before or when the change takes effect.

## Third-party services and external links

TechTips.fun may link to third-party websites, products, and services. When you follow an external link, the third party may place cookies or use other technologies under its own policies. We do not control those practices. Please review the third party’s cookie and privacy notices.
